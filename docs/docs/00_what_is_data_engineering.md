# What is Data Engineering?

## My Understanding

Data engineering is the discipline of building reliable systems that collect,
store, transform, and deliver data for business decisions, analytics, and AI.

## Supply-Chain Example

In a supply-chain shipment platform, data moves from carrier systems and
warehouse systems into a central platform. The data engineer ensures shipment
data is ingested correctly, validated, stored properly, transformed into
business-ready tables, and made available for dashboards.

## What a Data Engineer Owns

- Source-system understanding
- Data ingestion
- Data storage
- Data transformation
- Data quality
- Pipeline scheduling
- Monitoring and troubleshooting
- Documentation and lineage
- Cost and performance

## Why It Matters

Without reliable data engineering, business teams cannot trust reports,
data scientists cannot train reliable models, and operations teams cannot
make timely decisions.
